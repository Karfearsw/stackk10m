### Cross-cutting chain notes
- **Timeline/activity:** `createGlobalActivity` (leads, properties, calls, e-sign v1) and `createTeamActivityLog` (teams) feed `GET /api/activity` (13467) → `storage.getGlobalActivityLogs`. Contacts/buyers/tasks/contracts-email lack global-activity writes (P4 consistency gap, not breakage).
- **Investor portal flag:** entire portal gated by `INVESTOR_PORTAL_ENABLED` (default OFF, `server/investor/flag.ts:22`); all investor routes additionally pass `investorPortalGuard`.
